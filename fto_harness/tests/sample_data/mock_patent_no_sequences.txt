THERMOSTABLE ENZYME FOR INDUSTRIAL APPLICATION

Patent Number: WO2021987654A1
Applicant: Industrial Enzymes Corp.
Publication Date: 2021-08-15

FIELD OF THE INVENTION
The present invention relates to thermostable lipase enzymes for use in
detergent compositions.

BACKGROUND
Lipase B from Candida antarctica (CALB, EC 3.1.1.3) has been extensively
studied. The wild-type enzyme is available under UniProt accession P41365
and GenBank accession CAA83122.1.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION
The inventors have developed variants of CALB with enhanced thermostability
at temperatures above 70 degrees Celsius. The specific mutations are
described in reference to the wild-type sequence deposited under
UniProt accession P41365.

Key mutations include positions 103, 104, 221, and 278 of the mature
protein sequence.

Note: No explicit amino acid sequences are provided in this patent text.
The reader is directed to the publicly available database entries for the
wild-type sequence.

CLAIMS
1. A variant of the lipase having UniProt accession P41365, comprising
   at least one substitution selected from T103K, D104N, A221V, and G278S,
   wherein the variant has lipase activity and enhanced thermostability
   compared to the wild-type enzyme.
2. The variant of claim 1 having at least 85% sequence identity to
   the sequence deposited under UniProt accession P41365.
